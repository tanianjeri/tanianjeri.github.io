POWER BI | PUBLIC HEALTH

Global HIV Trends (2000–2024)

An analysis of HIV infections, mortality, treatment coverage and regional differences to explore global public health patterns.

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX

Key Findings

  • Annual new HIV infections declined from approximately 2.40 million in 2000 to 984,000 in 2024.
  • HIV-related deaths also declined over the study period.
  • Africa accounted for the largest share of people living with HIV in the 2024 dataset.
  • The analysis highlights the importance of expanding HIV prevention, testing, and treatment access.

Recommendations

Strengthen HIV prevention programmes, improve early diagnosis, and prioritise access to antiretroviral treatment in high-burden regions.
